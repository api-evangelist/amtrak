Amtrak seeks public feedback on a preliminary, management-developed framework designed to build on record momentum and position America's Railroad for the next 50 years of growth. Amtrak has never been stronger. Building on record ridership, record revenue, record capital investment, and more than $10 billion in new fleet investments, the Amtrak Board of Directors is advancing a preliminary framework, developed by Amtrak management, to modernize and transform the company's corporate structure.
